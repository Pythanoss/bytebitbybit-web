Privacy Policy — Magic Seal

Last updated: 22 November 2026 · Android app com.bytebitbybit.magicseal

What stays on your device

Your puzzle progress and settings (levels completed, stars, your daily streak, hints, audio and accessibility preferences) are saved in the app's private storage on your device.

What the app sends

To learn which puzzles are too hard and to fix crashes, Magic Seal uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics, which receive:

  • gameplay events: which puzzle you started or finished, how many moves and seconds it took, whether you used a hint, and your daily-puzzle streak;
  • crash reports: the error, the app version, and device details such as model and OS version;
  • a random app-instance identifier generated by Firebase, and coarse location derived from your IP address (country or city level).

This data is not linked to your name, email, or any account, and Analytics does not collect the Android advertising ID. Google processes it on our behalf under its privacy policy and keeps analytics data for up to 14 months. Remote settings that tune the game (for example how often ads may appear) are downloaded from Firebase Remote Config; nothing personal is sent for this.

Advertising

Magic Seal shows ads from Google AdMob: short videos you can choose to watch for a hint or to keep a daily streak, and occasional full-screen ads between puzzles (never in the first constellation). AdMob collects your device's advertising ID, IP address, and ad interactions to serve, measure and (where you allow it) personalise ads, and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android's settings. See how Google uses this data.

Purchases

You can buy Remove Ads, hint packs and the Patron of the sky bundle through Google Play. Buying requires signing in with Google (see below), so that what you buy, including hints, is kept with your account and follows you to any phone. Google Play handles payment; we receive only a record of what was bought, which the app uses to deliver it and keeps with your progress.

Turning analytics off

Settings → Privacy → Share anonymous usage stops all analytics and crash reporting immediately.

Signing in

Playing never requires an account. You can sign in with Google to keep your progress in the cloud and carry it to another phone, and signing in is required to make a purchase. We then store, in Google Firebase: your account's user ID, name and email as provided by Google, and a copy of your game progress (including what you bought). It is used only to restore your progress and purchases. We never post anything or read your contacts.

Deleting your account and data

  • Settings → Account → Delete account removes your account and cloud save immediately.
  • You can also email us (below) to have your account and cloud save deleted; we do so within 30 days.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all progress stored on the phone, and Settings → Reset progress erases it at any time.
  • To ask for analytics data to be deleted, email us with the approximate date you used the app.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your phone. Nothing is sent to a server.

What the app does not do

It does not read your contacts, precise location, files, camera or microphone.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If what the app collects changes, this policy will be updated before that version ships.
